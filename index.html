About

I work where product complexity meets production reality.

I’ve spent my career building business-critical systems across government, commerce, healthcare and operational software. I enjoy backend work that demands more than CRUD: workflow-heavy domains, payments, integrations, data consistency, performance, background processing, cloud infrastructure and reliable deployment.

Engineering philosophy

Simple interfaces. Strong boundaries. Observable systems. Calm production.

I prefer pragmatic architecture over novelty. The goal is software a team can understand, operate and evolve long after the first release.
